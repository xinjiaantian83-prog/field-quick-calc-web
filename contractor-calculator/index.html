For layout, not paperwork

Field Quick Calc is designed for measurements made at the jobsite. It helps organize inputs and return the dimensions most useful for layout, such as slope length, angle, pitch, radius, arc length, tread depth and tapered width.

Tools a contractor can reach quickly

The home screen puts Tapered Shape, Radius & Arc, Slope & Pitch and Stair Layout up front. Material references and notes remain one tap away.
