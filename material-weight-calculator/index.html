Weight and payload

Construction material weight calculator

Estimate material weight and the number of vehicle loads from volume, density and actual payload capacity.

The current app covers concrete, gravel, sand, asphalt and soil with metric and imperial volume, density and weight units. An optional payload input estimates exact and rounded-up load counts.

Field formulaestimated weight = volume × density · required loads = ceil(weight ÷ payload)

Supported measurements

Use m³, ft³ or yd³ for volume; kg/m³ or lb/ft³ for density; and kg, tonnes, pounds or U.S. tons for weight.

Payload-based load estimate

Enter the actual legal payload capacity of the vehicle to estimate the number of loads.

Reference values are not tickets

Density changes with material specification, moisture and compaction. Confirm supplier and vehicle data before loading.

Results are estimates only. Always follow local codes, project drawings, manufacturer specifications, site conditions and professional judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Which materials are included?

Concrete, gravel, sand, asphalt and soil are included in the current app.

Does the app know my vehicle limit?

No. Enter the actual payload capacity and verify legal and volume limits yourself.
