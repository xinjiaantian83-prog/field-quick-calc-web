Rebar estimator for wall layouts

Plan straight or L-shaped wall bars with cover, spacing, stock length and bar-size inputs.

The app's Rebar Estimator supports U.S. bar sizes #3 through #8 and metric sizes from 8 mm through 20 mm. It calculates piece count, cut length, pieces per stock bar, stock bars required, total length, estimated weight and remaining material.
