Pitch conversion

Pitch calculator for inches per foot

Turn measured rise and run into in/ft pitch and degrees without switching formulas.

In U.S. field work, pitch is often described as inches of vertical rise for every 12 inches of horizontal run. A 4 in/ft pitch rises 4 inches over each foot of run.

Field formulaPitch (in/ft) = rise (in) ÷ run (ft) · Angle = atan(pitch ÷ 12)

Pitch and slope are related

Pitch expresses the rise-per-foot ratio, while angle expresses the same incline in degrees. Slope length is the diagonal distance across the rise and run.

Example: reading a roof or cover pitch

Measure the vertical rise across a known horizontal run. Enter both values in the labeled fields to review pitch, angle and slope length together.

Field measurement matters

Do not use rafter or surface length as horizontal run. Confirm the measurement direction and the unit label before using the result for layout.

Results are estimates only. Always follow local codes, project drawings, manufacturer specifications, site conditions and professional judgment.

Frequently asked questions

What does 4 in/ft mean?

It means 4 inches of vertical rise for every 12 inches of horizontal run.

Is pitch the same as angle?

They describe the same incline differently. Pitch is a ratio; angle is measured in degrees.
